# HT Guide clinical audit — 26 Oct 2026

Sources checked against:
- **Thai CPG** = แนวทางการรักษาโรคความดันโลหิตสูงในเวชปฏิบัติทั่วไป พ.ศ. 2567 (Thai Hypertension Society, Guideline2024.pdf, 153 pages, thaihypertension.org)
- **EN CPG** = 2024 Thai guidelines on the treatment of hypertension, Asian Biomed 2025;19(6):316-357 (PMC12919385)
- **NLEM 2569** = บัญชียาหลักแห่งชาติ พ.ศ. 2569, group 2 Cardiovascular (Royal Gazette, same PDF the DM Guide uses)
- **Labels** = US prescribing information (DailyMed) / EU SmPC, from memory — to be linked per drug when fixed

Most guideline text in the app does trace to the Thai CPG (tables 5-44). The problems are below.

## A. Wrong against the Thai CPG (fix)

| # | Where | App says | CPG says |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Drug list: ARNI is filed under "First-Line Core Classes" and counted by the "1st-line" filter | first-line | ARNI is **not** a first-line class; may replace ACEI/ARB in resistant HT (IIb B), HFrEF (I A). Table 12 |
| A2 | Secondary HT > PA | Spironolactone target PRA > 1 is "Class I, Level B" | **IIb, B** (Table 38) |
| A3 | Crises > Aortic dissection | "within 20 minutes" | "as fast as possible" (เร็วที่สุดที่จะทำได้), Table 41 |
| A4 | Crises > Ischaemic stroke, no reperfusion | "reduce by 15% in 24 hours" | Rest briefly and re-measure; if still > 220/120, lower to SBP < 220 and DBP < 120 (IIa C). The 15% rule is AHA, not Thai CPG |
| A5 | Crises > sICH | "avoid BP variability (Class IIa, Level C)" in timeline only | correct grade, but text says lower SBP > 180 IV (IIa B) is missing |
| A6 | Starting > monotherapy rule | "Very elderly (>= 80y)" and "BP at risk with HIGH CV risk" | "advanced age" (no cut-off) and "BP at risk with **very high** CV risk" (Table 12) |
| A7 | Resistant > Step 2 | "Spironolactone if eGFR >= 30 and K+ <= 4.5" (and same as a hard rule on the spironolactone card) | Use MRA only if eGFR >= 30; use **with caution** if eGFR < 45 or K+ > 4.5 (Thai CPG p.128). Not a K+ cut-off |
| A8 | PSH > principles | pain relief graded IIb B (lumped with anxiolytics) | pain relief **IIa B**; anxiolytics IIb B (Table 42) |
| A9 | Comorbidities > Stroke | "ARB or ACEI + thiazide (PROGRESS/PATS)" | CPG: the size of BP reduction matters more than the drug class (I A); no class preferred |
| A10 | Comorbidities > CAD | "ACEI or ARB + Beta-blocker" | ACEI and/or beta-blocker; ARB only if ACEI not tolerated (Table 16) |

**Doses / label points to fix:**
- Eplerenone: app says contraindicated if CrCl < 30, max 50 mg/day. US label for **hypertension**: contraindicated if CrCl < 50, Cr > 2.0 (M) / > 1.8 (F), or T2DM with microalbuminuria; max 50 mg **twice daily**.
- Perindopril/amlodipine (Coveram): app says CrCl 30-60 "5/5 mg alternate days". SmPC: not suitable if CrCl < 60; titrate the separate drugs.
- Concor AM: lists 3 strengths but max 10/10 mg (the 10/10 strength is missing).
- Telmisartan "20 mg in hepatic impairment": EU SmPC says do not exceed 40 mg; no 20 mg start.
- Ramipril max 10 mg/day matches EU SmPC; US label allows 20 mg. Pick one source and say which.
